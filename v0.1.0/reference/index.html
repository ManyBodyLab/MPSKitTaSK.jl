MPSKitTaSK.MPSKitTaSK — Module

Tangent-space Krylov (TaSK) spectral functions of finite matrix product states, built on MPSKit.jl (arXiv:2510.07279).

source
MPSKitTaSK.LocalMPO — Type
LocalMPO(Ws::Vector{<:MPOTensor})

An operator acting on length(Ws) consecutive sites, given as a local MPO [W_1, …, W_N]. The left virtual leg of W_1 must be trivial; the right virtual leg of W_N is the auxiliary leg of the operator, which carries the excitation sector of charged operators.

MPSKitTaSK.TaSK — Type
TaSK(; krylovdim = 30, tol = MPSKit.Defaults.tol, verbosity = MPSKit.Defaults.verbosity, orth = KrylovKit.ModifiedGramSchmidt2(), kwargs...)

Tangent-space Krylov (TaSK) algorithm: a Lanczos recursion of the excitation Hamiltonian within the quasiparticle tangent space of a ground state (arXiv:2510.07279).

verbosity follows the MPSKit levels (0: none, 1: warnings, 2: start/finish, 3: every Lanczos step), reporting the lowest Ritz value (obj, the lowest excitation energy resolved so far) and its residual norm (err).

MPSKitTaSK.TaSKResult — Type
TaSKResult

Result of a TaSK Lanczos run: poles energies with spectral weights (summing to b0^2), the Krylov tridiagonal matrix T_kr, the norm b0 of the projected initial state, the ground-state expectation value expval, the initial vector phi0 and the Krylov basis.

MPSKitTaSK._local_op_projection — Function
_local_op_projection(Ws, gs, s::Int, left_gs = gs) -> (Φs, loc_ev)

Apply the local MPO Ws on sites s, …, s + N - 1 of gs (in mixed gauge around s) and return the effective one-site tensors Φs[i][l p; u r] at site s + i - 1, obtained by contracting all other sites with left_gs.AL (left) and left_gs.AR (right). The leg u is the auxiliary leg of W_N, which becomes the utility leg of the quasiparticle. Also returns ⟨gs|O|gs⟩ (zero for charged operators).

MPSKitTaSK.correlator_spectrum — Method
correlator_spectrum(times, C, ω_grid; σ = 0.05) -> Vector

Spectral function from a real-time correlator C(t) = ∑ₙ wₙ exp(-i Eₙ t) sampled at times (starting at 0): S(ω) = (1/π) Re ∫₀ᵀ C(t) exp(i ω t) exp(-σ² t² / 2) dt (trapezoidal rule). For σ T ≫ 1 this equals the Gaussian broadening of the poles with width σ, cf. gaussian_broadening.

MPSKitTaSK.dawson — Method
dawson(x::Real)

Dawson's integral D₊(x) = exp(-x²) ∫₀ˣ exp(t²) dt, accurate to machine precision: the Maclaurin series ∑ₙ x²ⁿ⁺¹ / (n! (2n + 1)) (all terms positive, so free of cancellation) for |x| ≤ 7 and the asymptotic expansion (1 / 2x) ∑ₖ (2k - 1)!! / (2x²)ᵏ beyond.

MPSKitTaSK.local_mpo — Method
local_mpo(O) -> Vector{<:MPOTensor}

Decompose an N-site operator O::AbstractTensorMap{T, S, N, N} into a local MPO [W_1, …, W_N] with trivial outer virtual legs, using MPSKit.decompose_localmpo(MPSKit.add_util_leg(O)). A LocalMPO is returned as is.

The left virtual leg of W_1 must be trivial. The right virtual leg of W_N is the auxiliary leg of the operator: trivial for symmetric operators, and carrying the excitation sector for charged ones (e.g. the spin-1 leg of spin_vector_operator).

MPSKitTaSK.project_tangent — Method
project_tangent(op, gs::MPSKit.AbstractFiniteMPS; site = nothing, momentum = nothing, kwargs...) -> (qp, b0, expval)

Project op|gs⟩ onto the one-site quasiparticle tangent space of the finite MPS gs and return the normalized tangent vector qp, its norm b0 and the ground-state expectation value expval = ⟨gs|op|gs⟩ (subtracted from the projection; zero for charged operators).

op may be an N-site AbstractTensorMap, a LocalMPO, a vector of such operators (one per site) or a FiniteMPO/FiniteMPOHamiltonian. Without momentum, a single operator is placed at site (default: the centre of the chain); with momentum = 2πn/L, it is summed over all sites with the plane-wave amplitudes exp(i k j) / √N. pos and k are aliases of site and momentum.

MPSKitTaSK.spin_vector_operator — Function
spin_vector_operator([elt = ComplexF64], [SU2Irrep]; spin = 1 // 2) -> LocalMPO

The spin vector operator 𝐒 as a one-site local MPO whose right virtual leg carries spin 1, obtained from the first factor of the local MPO of 𝐒ᵢ ⋅ 𝐒ⱼ and normalized to 𝐒 ⋅ 𝐒 = s(s+1). For an SU(2)-symmetric ground state, its spectral function is ∑ₐ Sᵃᵃ(k, ω) = 3 Sᶻᶻ(k, ω).

MPSKitTaSK.tdvp_structure_factor — Method
tdvp_structure_factor(H, O, gs, k_grid, ω_grid; σ = 0.05, dt = 0.1, tmax = 5 / σ, alg = TDVP(), envs = nothing, kwargs...)

Momentum-resolved dynamical structure factor from TDVP time evolution of the projected states O_k|gs⟩ within the tangent space, Fourier transformed with a Gaussian window of width σ (see correlator_spectrum). This is directly comparable to the Gaussian broadening of the TaSK poles from dynamical_structure_factor. Remaining keyword arguments are passed to project_tangent. Returns a named tuple with k_grid, k_over_pi, omega_grid, times, S_kw (ω × k) and the correlators C_k(t).
